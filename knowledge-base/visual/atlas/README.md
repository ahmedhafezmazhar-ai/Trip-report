# Knowledge map (atlas version)

This is the source of the published knowledge map: https://claude.ai/artifact/SPgL6s1bXEUb3antygXsoo

- `knowledge-map.html` is the page and `map.json` is the tree of families, branches and pages. `build.py` builds the PsychonautWiki part.
- `text/42.json` and `text/43.json` hold the TiHKAL and Shulgin Index entries.
- `text/00.json`–`41.json` hold the PsychonautWiki article text.
- `_tools/merge_books_map.py` adds the "Shulgin books" family to a `map.json` fresh from `build.py`.

The older radial map is kept in `../knowledge-map.html` with `../tree.json`.
