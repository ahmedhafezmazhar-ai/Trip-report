# Hallucinogens › Aborting trips

[Hallucinogens](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Hallucinogens) have the potential to become overwhelming and push the user into a paranoid or dreadful mood, particularly if they are inexperienced or in an inappropriate [set and setting](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Set_and_setting).

If one decides to terminate the trip, [benzodiazepines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Benzodiazepines) and other [sedatives](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Sedatives) such as some [antipsychotics](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Antipsychotics) can be considered analogous to an "eject button" of a downhill-headed or extensively long trip. These substances tend to be very effective tools in preventing [panic attacks](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Anxiety), [paranoia](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Paranoia), and possible traumatic experiences.

However, experienced users generally advise trying to wait out difficult parts of a trip if possible. Challenging moments are often temporary and can turn out to be catalysts for the greatest learning experiences.
